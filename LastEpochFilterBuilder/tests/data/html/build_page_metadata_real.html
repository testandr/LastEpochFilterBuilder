The Shadow Rend Bladedancer unleashes storms of spinning blades, carving through enemies in a razor-sharp whirlwind. Each Shadow Rend creates Shadows that cast Shadow Cascade, creating an explosive misty field that destroys anything caught within.

This build's main Damage comes from the Shadow Cascades cast by your Shadow Rend thanks to the Shadow Massacre Node. It is crucial to stay inside your Smoke Bomb to take advantage of the Sinister Plan Node to properly sustain Mana.

The gearing for this build is advanced as it needs many Uniques, Set and +Shadows pieces to function smoothly. Items like Tabi of Dusk and Dawn, Traitor's Tongue, Kuzon's Guise, Firestarter's Torch and Legends Entwined turn this build into an absolute powerhouse. Become almost immortal abusing the Ward Gained When You Create a Shadow Affix.

The Shadow Rend Bladedancer is a powerful build that has insane AoE and Damage potential. If you're looking for an unethically powerful build that provides a PoE-like experience in Last Epoch, this setup is perfect for you!

Gameplay

Season 4 - Shattered Omens
Shadow Rend Bladedancer

Skill Usage/Rotation

  • Cast Smoke Bomb.
  • Spam Shadow Rend at your target while inside Smoke Bomb.
  • Watch the Shadow Cascades cast by your Shadow Rend kill everything.
  • Move around the battlefield with traversal Shift and continue spamming Shadow Rend.
  • Since Mana sustain is very tight, it's not recommended to self cast any other Skills.

Bossing

  • Take advantage of all your Defensive and Offensive Buffs and keep them active.
  • Remember to stay inside your Smoke Bomb at all times.

    • Keep track of your Silver Shroud stacks. This mechanic is a powerful defensive tool which guarantees to Dodge the next hit. This proves to be great especially against Boss mechanics that can be Dodged, enabling you to ignore them completely when you have Silver Shroud up.
